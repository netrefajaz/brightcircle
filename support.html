Need help right now?

If someone is in danger or not breathing, call 911. In crisis in the US, call or text 988. The SAMHSA National Helpline (1 800 662 4357) is free, confidential, and open 24/7, and family members can call too.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts and no Bright Circle server holding your data. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

With Bright Circle Plus, go to Settings → Backup and restore → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, like iCloud Drive or email. On the new phone, install Bright Circle and choose Restore from a backup on the welcome screen.

What's included in Bright Circle Plus?

Hard moment support, the Stay safe and Know their pattern skills, daily check ins, boundaries, finding support, your journal, reasons, support circle, gratitude, and several Learn articles are always free. Plus adds insights and charts, boundary insights, the full Learn library, the monthly summary, journal prompt packs, search and export, a look back at past weeks, and backups.

How do I cancel Plus?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Bright Circle.

Can my loved one see what I write?

Not unless they have your phone. You can turn on the app lock in Settings so Face ID or your passcode is needed to open Bright Circle, and turn on discreet reminders so nothing personal shows on your lock screen.

Reminders aren't showing up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Bright Circle in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. Then turn the reminder off and on again in the app.

Is Bright Circle connected to Al-Anon or Nar-Anon?

No. Bright Circle isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Al-Anon, Nar-Anon, SMART Recovery, or any other organization. The app links to their meeting finders so you can find support.

Can Bright Circle replace therapy or treatment?

No. It's a self help tool to use alongside real support. A therapist who understands addiction in families, or a family support group, can help in ways an app can't.
