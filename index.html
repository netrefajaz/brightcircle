When someone you love is struggling, this space is for you.

Bright Circle is a private companion for family and friends of people living with addiction. It helps you get through hard moments, hold your boundaries, learn what's really going on, and remember to take care of yourself. No accounts, no ads, and everything stays on your phone.

What's inside

  • Hard moment support with breathing, grounding, and your support circle one tap away
  • Boundaries: write them down, then log each time you hold one
  • Daily check ins for mood and worry, with gentle insights over time
  • A private journal with prompts written for family members
  • Family skills: a safety plan with the domestic violence hotline, and knowing their pattern
  • Log the family group meetings you go to
  • Short, plain language articles on enabling, relapse, treatment, and overdose safety
  • Links to family support groups, treatment search, and helplines
  • A This week card and a Progress tab that shows how your skills are going, never a day count
  • An optional Face ID or passcode lock, and discreet reminders

Bright Circle Plus

Five more family skills, based on CRAFT: talk so they can hear you, make sober time feel good, step back when they're using, take care of you, and invite them to treatment. Each has practice prompts, a log of every try, and a PDF to share with a therapist or counselor.

Plus also includes insights into what helps you and when hard moments hit, boundary insights, the full Learn library, a monthly summary to share, journal prompt packs, a look back at past weeks, and backups.

In recovery yourself? Bright Circle has a sister app, Bright Days, built for the person in recovery.
