Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you add to Bright Circle is stored on your phone. There are no accounts, and we don't have a server that holds your data.
  • We don't use analytics, ads, trackers, or data brokers, and we never sell or share your information.
  • The app doesn't use your location and doesn't send anything over the internet. It only hands you off to your browser, phone, or messages when you tap a link, call, or text button.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything in it.

Bright Circle is a companion app for family and friends of people living with addiction, made by Jasmine Netrefa-Drinkwater ("we," "us"). What you're going through is personal, and this policy explains plainly what the app does with your information. If anything here is unclear, email brightcircleapp@gmail.com.

When the app connects to anything

Bright Circle works fully offline and doesn't send your information anywhere. Some buttons hand you off to another app: support group and treatment links open in your browser, and call and text buttons open your phone or messages app. Once you leave Bright Circle, those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

If you buy Bright Circle Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your name, email, or payment details.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent from a server. "Discreet reminders" keep anything personal off your lock screen.
  • Photos: only when you choose to add a photo to a reason. The app only gets the photos you pick.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. Your phone checks it; Bright Circle never receives your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

Sharing, exports, and backups

Some features create a file or image: skill PDFs, journal PDFs, the monthly summary, and backups. These are only created when you tap the button, and they go wherever you send them through your phone's share sheet (for example Files, iCloud Drive, email, or a message).

Backups contain everything in the app, including your journal and photos, and they are not encrypted. Keep backup files somewhere private, and only share them with people you trust.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, crash tracking, advertising, or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No AI or automated review of your journal or check ins. Insights are simple counts and patterns worked out on your phone.

Deleting your information

Because your information only lives on your phone, you're in control. Delete individual entries in the app, or delete Bright Circle from your phone to erase everything. If you made backup files, delete those wherever you saved them. We have no copy to delete.

Age

Bright Circle is for adults 18 and older (see the Terms of Use). We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical care

Bright Circle is education and support (see the Terms of Use). It isn't medical, psychological, legal, or emergency care, and it doesn't replace a doctor, therapist, or treatment program. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988, or call 911 if someone is in danger.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Bright Circle handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's update notes.
